Why this project exists

Your energy history should remain yours

After SunPower Corporation filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in August 2024, monitoring for many existing systems transitioned to SunStrong Connect. Access to features such as historical performance, analytics, and panel-level monitoring is now part of a paid premium plan for many system owners. Solar Unlocked gives owners of compatible PVS6 systems another option: an inexpensive ESP32-S3 touchscreen that collects and stores solar data locally on a microSD card. Owners can view live and historical information on the touchscreen or open the dashboard from a browser on the same home network. The firmware is completely free and open source.

Background: SunPower bankruptcy announcement and SunStrong monitoring information.

Hardware

A small, affordable build

No computer or server needs to run alongside the finished device. Gather these four items before installing the firmware.

01

ESP32-S3 touchscreen module

Use the LCDWiki 3.5-inch ESP32-S3 Display, model ES3C35P. The supported board has 16 MB flash, 8 MB PSRAM, a 320 × 480 capacitive-touch IPS screen, an ST77922 display controller, USB-C, and a microSD slot.

02

microSD card

Use a reputable card with at least 8 GB capacity, formatted as FAT32. A 16–32 GB high-endurance card is recommended for reliable, continuous history recording.

03

USB data cable

Use a USB-A-to-USB-C or USB-C-to-USB-C cable that supports data. Charge-only cables can power the board but cannot install the firmware.

04

5 V USB power supply

Choose a regulated, good-quality USB power supply suitable for continuous touchscreen, Wi‑Fi, and microSD operation.

Legacy PVS6 systems only

Solar Unlocked is designed for legacy SunPower systems using a PVS6 monitoring device. PVS5 systems and other solar gateways are not compatible.

Browser-based installer

Install the firmware over USB

Connect a supported display to a desktop computer with a data-capable USB cable. No development tools or command line are required.

For ESP32-S3 with 3.5" display (ES3C35P)
Use desktop Chrome or Edge to install. Open this page over HTTPS to enable USB access.

Works on Windows, macOS, Linux, and ChromeOS with a data-capable USB cable.

!

Before you install

This installation erases the ESP32 flash, including saved Wi‑Fi and device settings. Files on a removable microSD card are not touched. Disconnect other serial devices before continuing.

How to use

Set up your monitor after installation

Keep the touchscreen, PVS6, and the computer or phone used for the dashboard on the same home network.

  1. 01

    Connect the PVS6 to home Wi‑Fi

    In the SunStrong Connect app, open Profile, choose WiFi Settings, and follow the prompts to connect the PVS6 to your 2.4 GHz home network.

  2. 02

    Find the PVS6 address and serial suffix

    Open your router’s app or administration page and look in its connected-device or DHCP list for the PVS6; record its local IP address. Find the PVS6 serial number on the unit’s label and record its final five characters exactly. Treat these details as private. If the label is not safely visible, contact your installer rather than opening electrical equipment.

  3. 03

    Configure Solar Unlocked

    Open Settings on the touchscreen. Select your home Wi‑Fi, enter its password, then enter the PVS6 IP address and final five serial characters. Select your timezone and tap Save & Connect. Watch the status area at the top of the screen; after Wi‑Fi and PVS6 connections succeed, open Live to confirm readings appear.

  4. 04

    Start recording history

    With the device powered off, insert a good-quality FAT32 microSD card, then power it back on. Valid energy readings are recorded automatically. After a few hours, open History to see accumulated data points. The Panels page shows live information for individual solar panels.

  5. 05

    Open the browser dashboard

    On a phone or computer connected to the same home network, visit http://mypvs.local in a browser to view or download your locally stored data.

Troubleshooting

If the device is not detected

  • Use desktop Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge; Safari and Firefox do not currently expose Web Serial.
  • Try another USB cable—many charging cables do not carry data.
  • Close serial monitors and other apps that may already be using the port.
  • If prompted, hold the device's BOOT button while connecting, then release it.
